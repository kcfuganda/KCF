# How to post a story on kcfuganda.org

This guide is for the KCF team in Uganda. It explains how to add stories and photos to the website yourselves, from a phone or a computer. Only people who have been invited can do this.

## The first time

1. Look in your email for an invitation from **Pages CMS**. Open it and follow the link.
2. Sign in with the same email address the invitation was sent to.
3. You will see the KCF website and a section called **Stories**.

After that, you can always go straight to **https://app.pagescms.org** and sign in.

## Write a new story

1. Open **Stories** and press **Add an entry** (it may also be called **New** or have a **+** button).
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Story title.** A short name, for example "The new chicks have arrived".
   - **Date.** The day the story happened, or today.
   - **Show this story on the website.** Leave this switched on when you are ready for everyone to see it. Switch it off to keep a draft private.
   - **Short summary.** One or two sentences. These appear on the story card on the homepage.
   - **Main photo** and **Main photo caption.** The picture at the top of the story. Any shape is fine. Tall photos, wide photos, and square photos are all shown whole, never cut off.
   - **The story.** Write it the way you would tell it to a friend. You can make words bold, add headings, and make lists.
   - **More photos.** Press the button to add a photo, then write about it in **About this photo**. You can write as much as you like: who is in the photo, what was happening, why it matters. Leave an empty line between paragraphs. Add as many photos as you want.
3. Press **Save**.

The website updates by itself. The new story usually appears within 2 to 5 minutes. If you don't see it, wait a little, then refresh the page.

## Change or remove a story

- **To fix a mistake:** open the story in **Stories**, make the change, and press **Save**.
- **To hide a story without deleting it:** switch off **Show this story on the website** and press **Save**.
- **To delete a story completely:** open the story and choose **Delete**.

## Tips for good photos

- Photos taken on a phone work well. If a photo is very large, sending it to yourself on WhatsApp first makes it smaller and quicker to load, without losing much quality.
- Hold the phone steady and make sure faces are in the light.
- Please only share photos of children in ways that protect their dignity and privacy. Avoid photos where a child is undressed, distressed, or could be put at risk. If in doubt, leave it out.

## The practice story

There is a story called **"Example story (not shown on the website)"**. It is switched off, so the public cannot see it. You can open it to see how the fields look, edit it into a real story, or delete it.

## Need help?

Contact Vince. The editor can only change stories, not the rest of the website, and every change is saved in the site's history, so mistakes can always be undone.
